BREAKING

Trump declares Iran deal 'now complete' as naval blockade ends

President Trump announced Sunday that "the deal with the Islamic Republic of Iran is now complete" and immediately authorized the removal of the US naval blockade from the Strait of Hormuz, PBS, RFERL and NBC News report. The formal signing ceremony is set for June 19 in Switzerland. G7 leaders will convene at Evian to lock in a long-term Hormuz security framework, CNBC reports, but Israeli strikes in Lebanon are raising fears the accord could unravel before it is formally signed, CBS News reports.

The Strait of Hormuz carries roughly 20% of global oil — the naval blockade ending is the biggest single geopolitical shift in the Gulf in years and will directly affect energy costs from Dubai to Delhi.

Source: PBS, RFERL, NBC News, CBS News, CNBC · 12:00 GST
MONEY

Oil falls below $80 as Iran deal betting turns to selling

Brent crude dropped 5.72% on Sunday to around $80 a barrel, its lowest in years, TradingEconomics and CNBC report, extending a month-long fall of more than 23% as markets price in the lifting of the US naval blockade and Iranian oil sanctions. Energy experts caution that the actual supply recovery will be slow — Iranian export infrastructure has deteriorated under years of sanctions, and Hormuz shipping lanes need safety verification before full commercial traffic can resume.

Cheaper crude is immediate relief for Gulf consumers and India's import bill, but traders are already pricing in supply that may take months to fully return.

Source: TradingEconomics, CNBC · 12:00 GST
WORLD

Russia strikes Kyiv Pechersk Lavra and three other cities; at least 10 killed

Russia launched a large-scale ballistic missile and drone strike across Ukraine overnight on June 14–15, killing at least 5 people and injuring 29 in Kyiv — including children aged 5 and 6 — and directly striking the Kyiv Pechersk Lavra, one of Ukraine's most sacred monastery complexes and a UNESCO-listed cultural heritage site, the Kyiv Independent reports. Separately, five emergency workers were killed and four injured in Kharkiv; Russian drones also struck Sumy, injuring three including a child, and destroyed a college building in Dnipro. The attacks are part of an escalating June pattern: a June 2 assault killed at least 22 civilians; a June 11 Kharkiv strike killed 3 and injured 64.

Moscow is escalating its air campaign even as the world's attention shifts to the Gulf — and is now targeting Ukraine's most revered religious and cultural landmarks.

Source: Kyiv Independent, NPR, Euronews · 14:30 GST
SPORT

World Cup Day 5: Brazil draws Morocco; Australia shocks Turkey

Brazil and Morocco played out a tense 1-1 draw in Group D on June 15 as Australia delivered one of the tournament's early upsets by defeating Turkey, Yahoo Sports and SBS News report. The results follow a wild June 14 that saw Germany thrash Curaçao 7-1 — Kai Havertz bagged a brace — and the Netherlands held to a 2-2 draw by Japan, with Daichi Kamada equalizing in the 88th minute.

Australia's win and Brazil's draw show that the expanded 48-team format is delivering genuine upsets — the tournament's old hierarchy has no guaranteed path.

Source: Yahoo Sports, SBS News, ESPN · 12:00 GST
MONEY

India's RBI holds rates at 5.25% and trims GDP outlook to 6.6%

The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee voted unanimously on June 5 to hold the repo rate at 5.25% with a neutral stance, and trimmed its FY2026-27 GDP growth forecast from 6.9% to 6.6%, citing global energy-price disruption and weak merchandise export demand. With Hormuz now reopening and oil prices already down 23% this month, both cited constraints may be easing — analysts are watching the next MPC meeting for a possible rate cut.

The two headwinds the RBI cited when it cut the growth forecast — energy costs and export softness — are now shifting. If oil stays down, a rate cut becomes more likely at the next MPC review.

Source: Reserve Bank of India, TradingEconomics · Jun 5 decision · 12:00 GST
