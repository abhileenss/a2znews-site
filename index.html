Trump says a US-Iran deal signs today; Iran disputes the timing

President Trump said the US and Iran will sign an interim deal today to end the 2026 war, with the Strait of Hormuz reopening 'to all' immediately after, Bloomberg, NPR and CNBC report. The expected 60-day memorandum would lift the US naval blockade, waive oil sanctions and release about $25bn in frozen Iranian assets. Iran's foreign ministry said a Sunday signing is unlikely and disputed the timing, while Qatari mediators flew to Tehran to close the gap. As of 19:00 GST nothing is signed, so we are keeping this Developing until both sides actually sign.

A real deal would reopen the world's most important oil chokepoint and pull the Gulf back from the edge of a wider war.

MONEY

Oil falls to a three-month low as a peace deal looks closer

Brent crude fell more than 4% on Friday to around $86 a barrel, its lowest since early March, TradingEconomics and CNBC report, as traders priced in a possible US-Iran agreement. Analysts tie the slide to a draft that would lift Iranian oil sanctions and reopen Hormuz within 30 days. Markets are closed over the weekend; the next print is Monday.

Cheaper crude eases fuel and inflation pressure from the Gulf to India, but only while the deal optimism holds.

Source: TradingEconomics, CNBC, Gulf News · 18:15 GST

Drake's Iceman holds No. 1 after historic Billboard top-three sweep

Drake's ICEMAN spent a third straight week atop the Billboard 200 with about 171,000 equivalent album units, Billboard reports. BUSINESS

GSK buys cancer drugmaker Nuvalent in $10.6bn oncology bet

Britain's GSK agreed to acquire US cancer-drug developer Nuvalent for $10.6 billion in all cash, about $124 a share and a 40% premium, GSK and Bloomberg report. It is GSK's largest deal in more than a decade, adding two late-stage lung-cancer drugs as CEO Luke Miels leans into oncology. Nuvalent shares jumped about 39%; the deal targets a Q3 2026 close, subject to regulatory and tender conditions.

Big Pharma is spending billions to lock up cancer pipelines, shaping which treatments reach patients next.

Source: GSK, Bloomberg, CNBC · 18:15 GST
